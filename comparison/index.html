TL;DR

Three platforms, three philosophies. Here's the quick verdict:

  • kube-llmops — Batteries-included, cloud-agnostic, one-command install. Best if you want everything out of the box.
  • KAITO — Solid Microsoft project, but Azure-only. Good for Azure shops.
  • KServe — Standardized, mature, but requires significant setup (Knative, ISTIO, etc.).

When to Choose Each

Choose kube-llmops if...

  • You want a one-command, batteries-included LLM platform
  • You need multi-team support with key management, rate limiting, budget tracking
  • You want observability out of the box
  • You are deploying RAG or fine-tuning pipelines
  • You are not on Azure (or want multi-cloud flexibility)

Choose KAITO if...

  • You are on Azure and want deep integration
  • You want a CNCF Sandbox project backed by Microsoft

Choose KServe if...

  • You need a standardized, production-grade inference platform
  • You are already in the Knative / ISTIO ecosystem
  • You prefer a modular, bring-your-own-components approach
